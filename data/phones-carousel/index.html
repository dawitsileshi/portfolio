Phones Without Accounts: social carousel

Seven slides that take my insight brief from a two-page PDF to a phone screen, with its caveats intact, in English and Amharic.

Format
7 slides, 1080 × 1350 px, for Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn
Languages
English and Amharic, each a full set
Built in
Figma
Data
World Bank Global Findex 2025 microdata, surveyed areas only. Read the brief

Scroll sideways, or open a slide for the full-size image.

How it was made

Why a carousel. The brief is a two-page PDF: right for a partner meeting, wrong for a phone. I wanted the same finding, with its caveats, in the format most people would actually see it: a swipeable post, in Amharic as well as English.

Starting point. The layout began as a reference mockup I made with AI tools from the brief's verified figures, with an exact position for every bar and dot. I built all fourteen slides in Figma from that reference.

The Amharic version. I wrote it rather than translating line by line. The hardest word was "interval": the usual term, ክልል, also means "region", and the same slides name the regions the survey left out. I used የስህተት ህዳግ ("margin of error") instead. Ethiopic headlines also run longer, four lines where English needs three, so the Amharic slides needed their own line breaks.

Checking the export. After exporting, every bar and dot was measured against the verified figures with a script (AI-assisted). The check caught marks drawn off-scale and a caveat that said the opposite of the brief, and I corrected them before publishing.

Scope. Findex 2025 did not interview in Amhara, Tigray, Somali or Harari, about 30% of the population, so every slide says "adults surveyed", never "Ethiopian adults".

Next. Show it to a few people on their phones before posting, and make an A4 print version for meetings.
