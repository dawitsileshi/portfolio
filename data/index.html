How this work was made

I loaded the survey data into PostgreSQL and checked the weighting against published figures before any analysis. Ethiopia's weighted figures match the World Bank's published values (48.8% account ownership, 9.5% mobile money, 20.7% digital payments).

I worked with an AI assistant (Claude) on the analysis plan, queries, chart code and copy, and reran every figure in SQL myself. The SQL files on this page reproduce them.

Data: World Bank, Global Findex Database 2025 (doi.org/10.48529/bk9n-8r43). Independent analysis, not affiliated with the World Bank or any company named in the data.
