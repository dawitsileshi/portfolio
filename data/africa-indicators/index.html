Method and limits

Validation. Ethiopia's figures match the published Findex 2025 values: account ownership 48.8%, mobile money 9.5%, digital payments 20.7%. The same code produces every country.

Country figures use each country's survey weight. The regional figure rescales each country's weights to its adult population, so large countries count more, as they should for a "share of African adults" figure.

Margins are approximate 95% intervals from the effective sample size of the weights. Clustering isn't in the public file, so true margins are wider. Countries a few points apart are often not different.

Limits. One survey round, about 1,000 adults per country. Mauritania was not asked about mobile money. These are shares of adults, not counts of accounts; Ethiopia's 135 million-plus wallets are accounts, many of them unused or held by the same person.

Source: World Bank, Global Findex Database 2025 (doi.org/10.48529/bk9n-8r43). Independent analysis by Dawit Sileshi. Not affiliated with the World Bank or Shega.
